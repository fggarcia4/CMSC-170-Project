Perceptron

Because accuracy was low, we adjusted the learning rate to see whether it made a real difference. We tried five rates (0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 and 0.5), from small to relatively large, to see how step size affects convergence, classification performance and training time. We used 0.1 as the baseline, and every rate gave results similar to it.

Results and discussion

For both models we went from 3 inputs to 8 by adding 5 more.

Naive Bayes

Accuracy rose from 68.09% to 68.98%. The Macro-F1 improvement was much more visible, going from 0.4648 to 0.6619.

Perceptron

Accuracy rose from 55.37% to 64.26%. Macro-F1 again improved more visibly, from 0.4397 to 0.5945.
